# Interview questions
1. Why FastAPI? — Async-friendly APIs, validation and OpenAPI.
2. Why asyncio? — Concurrent I/O for telemetry and agent tools.
3. Why LangGraph? — Explicit stateful, controllable workflows.
4. What is RAG? — Retrieve evidence then generate grounded output.
5. Why hybrid retrieval? — Semantic relevance plus exact operational terms.
6. What is BM25? — Sparse lexical ranking.
7. Why Qdrant? — Vector search with filtering.
8. Why Isolation Forest? — Unsupervised anomaly detection.
9. What is contamination? — Expected outlier proportion.
10. Why PostgreSQL? — Durable relational incident state.
11. Why Redis? — Fast transient state/queue backend.
12. Why Celery? — Background jobs and retries.
13. What is SRE? — Reliability engineering using measurable service objectives.
14. Why human approval? — Prevent unsafe autonomous actions.
15. What is tool authorization? — Policy gate before operational side effects.
16. Why simulation? — Safe reproducible demos.
17. What is OpenTelemetry? — Vendor-neutral telemetry instrumentation.
18. Prometheus role? — Metrics collection/querying.
19. Grafana role? — Visualization and alerting.
20. What makes RCA evidence-backed? — Explicit observed evidence references.
21. How prevent prompt injection? — Treat retrieved text as untrusted data.
22. Why JWT? — Stateless authenticated API access.
23. Why RBAC? — Least privilege by role.
24. Why recursive loops? — Re-investigate when verification fails, with a hard cap.
25. What is verification? — Compare service health before/after action.
26. Why provider abstraction? — Swap local simulation for real infrastructure safely.
27. What is structured output? — Schema-constrained machine-readable agent result.
28. What should be measured? — Detection, retrieval, RCA, remediation and verification metrics.
29. What is a deployment regression? — A release that worsens service behavior.
30. How would you scale this? — Separate ingestion, workers, vector store and API horizontally with durable queues.
